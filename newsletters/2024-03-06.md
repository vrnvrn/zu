# Zuzalu Community Newsletter | March 2024 Edition

 Happy March, Zuzalu!

 After a short month, we have a short but important newsletter for you today.
 Welcome to the **March 2024 Edition of the Zuzalu newsletter**,
 where you’ll read about the results of the recent Quadratic Funding round and
 **open questions that need your attention**, along with the usual
 Zuzagora trending topics, community accomplishments and reflections, and
 upcoming events.

## I. **Zuzalu Quadratic Funding﻿QF Round Action Items & Anonymous Concerns Thread**

 The **Technology**
 and **Events**
 Quadratic Funding rounds have closed, and today **Umar shared an in-depth
 analysis** of the results with several options for how to
 proceed.

 There are two key decisions that need to be made for both rounds: whose
 donations should be matched and what matching algorithm should be used.

 All eligible voters could be matched, only verified Zuzalu residents, or other
 criteria. The algorithm could follow the original **quadratic
 funding** matching, **pairwise
 matching**, **cluster
 matching**, or in theory, something else.

 **﻿These decisions will significantly impact the amount of funds that
 projects will receive.** For example, this chart visualizes the
 difference if only verified Zuzalu residents (aka donation addresses with a
 POAP) are matched:

 What do you think? Please take a moment to read over the **post** and
 share your opinions.

 Additionally, the **Zuzagora team opened an
 anonymous thread** two weeks ago for sharing concerns about
 the QF round. The conversation has been mostly about eligibility of events so
 far; feel free to use it if you’d also like to anonymously comment on how you
 think the matching criteria and algorithm should be defined.
