## **III. Quadratic Funding Q1 Final Results**

 Thank you all for the engagement in discussing the QF round! In case you missed
 it, the final decision was to go with a hybrid option, using Cluster Matching
 for all donations and Pairwise Matching for donations that claimed the Zuzalu
 POAP.

 The top 3 matching amounts for the Tech Round were Zupass, MuseMatrix, and
 Social Layer.

 The top 3 matching amounts for the Events Round were Edge City, Blockravers Q1
 - Vitalia + Colorado, and Vitalia.

 **You can read the full
 thread and matching amounts for each project here.**
