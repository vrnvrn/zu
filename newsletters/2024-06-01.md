# Zuzalu Community Newsletter | June 2024 Edition

 And just like that, another month has gone by! It's a busy time for many of us
 with an abundance of ongoing and upcoming events, and it's hard to believe we
 just wrapped up Zuzalu one year ago. I swear, sometimes it feels like we're on
 an accelerated timeline!

 Welcome to the **June 2024 Edition of the Zuzalu newsletter**,
 where you’ll read Zuzalu.city tech updates, Zuzagora trending topics, community
 accomplishments and reflections, and upcoming events.

 *Zanzalu Participants*

## II. Zuzalu.City

 We are thrilled to share some of the latest developments and initiatives at
 Zuzalu City.

 🌟 Introducing ZuChat! We are excited to introduce ZuChat, now available on
 YouTube, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify. Hosted by Zuzalu.City, ZuChat brings
 together the builders, dreamers, and doers of ZuVillages for insightful
 discussions on topics ranging from technology to community governance.

 So far, three episodes are live:

 1. **Co-Building Zuzalu.City
 with Lemonade, with KC from lemonade.**

 2. **Enhancing Sustainability
 Through Rational Resources Allocation, with Daniela from Blockful and Nico from
 LottoPGF.**

 3. **The Development Process of
 Zuzalu.City and Carbonvote, with Reno from ECF.**

 As a community-driven initiative, we want your input. What topics are you
 passionate about? What issues deserve more attention? Join the conversation and
 help us shape a decentralized, community-driven podcast. Join ZuChat on on
 **YouTube**,
 **Apple
 Podcasts**, and **Spotify**.

 🚀 ZuCity MVP Development. The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of Zuzalu.City is
 under development and will be live soon. Stay tuned for more updates!

 🎤 ZuCity at ETHNA. ZuCity recently gave a speech at ETHNA on "How Zuzalu.City
 Can Empower Communities in Italy." This talk shared potential of ZuCity to
 enhance network states and empower local communities in Italy. Stay tuned for
 the video link when it becomes available!

 💚 Giveth QF for ZuCity. ZuCity recently participated in the Giveth QF round,
 which has just concluded. A huge shoutout to everyone who supported us during
 this round and to LottoPGF for nominating ZuCity. Your contributions are
 greatly appreciated!

 💗 Building Reputation and Trust with Blockful. Blockful and Zuzalu.city are
 discussing creating attestations and localized reputations, allowing users to
 issue and verify score badges to present their reputation. More collabs are on
 the way!

 Together, we are building a vibrant, innovative, and collaborative community.
