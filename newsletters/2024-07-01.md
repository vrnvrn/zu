 **Edge Esmeralda**

 Edge Esmeralda is officially wrapped up! It was an amazing time — thank you to
 everyone in the Zuzalu community who came and participated. We will share a
 more detailed wrap up soon, but suffice to say that it was a wonderful, weird
 mind-expanding, relationship-building, and fun month ☀️

 Some fun stats, courtesy of **Social
 Layer**: we had 551 sessions on the calendar throughout the
 month (average of 18 per day), with 167 unique hosts or cohosts, out of the
 1,000 or so people who came. It was so cool to see emergent programming really
 working.

 One of the most gratifying things was the integration with the local community
 in the town of Healdsburg. One of the local reporters wrote a thoughtful piece
 about it, which you can read here, where she wrote: “Edge Esmeralda was one of
 the weirdest, wildest, most ambitious, most unexpected — and most fun — things
 I’ve ever watched unfold in Healdsburg.”

 We’ll have more info to come after we have a moment to retro everything,

 **ZuBledThis September, ZuBled brings the Zuzalu community to the
 Julian Alps for a month-long gathering in Bled, Slovenia. Set against the
 backdrop of pristine lakes and mountains, ZuBled focuses on advancing key areas
 of innovation: longevity and health, decentralized science, real-world crypto
 applications, network states, AI, and space tech. The unique Alpine setting
 serves as both inspiration and testing ground, with SubWork coworking hub just
 steps away from Lake Bled. ZuBled offers a perfect blend of focused work
 sessions, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and nature-inspired thinking. Join
 70 long-term residents and 100 short-term visitors in shaping the future of
 technology and society in one of Europe's most beautiful locations. To keep you
 posted, **register for ZuBled on
 Luma**. 

 **﻿ZuIndia**

 Zu-India, scheduled for November to mid-December 2024 (tentative) in
 Bangalore-India, is an experimental pop-up village inspired by Zuzalu. It aims
 to tackle India's core challenges by avoiding long-term undesirable equilibrium
 states. The initiative focuses on three fundamental challenges: talent,
 ambition, and capital. Zu-India seeks to cultivate truth-seeking, encourage
 critical thinking, and foster a culture of ambition and defensive risk-taking.
 Zu-India will experiment with governance models to address systemic challenges
 in India. It aims to drive both local and global problem-solving, promoting
 innovation through cross-pollination among different fields of technology, art,
 and culture.

## II. Zuzalu.City

 **Moving from Guilded to Element Since all Guilded accounts will require a Roblox account
 connection to maintain access from July 15th, ZuCity is transitioning our
 collaboration space from Guilded to Element. Our Status community is still in
 beta testing, but if you're curious and want to check it out, feel free to join
 **there**,
 too. For now, we'll be settling into Element for a smoother experience.
 **Join us on
 Element** to continue being part of our vibrant community. Thanks
 for rolling with us through this transition! 

 **Current Features for Zucity MVP**

- NFT Tickets • Building with Scrollpass: Zuzalu.city is leveraging
 Scrollpass to build and integrate NFT tickets, offering a secure and verifiable
 method for event access.

- Funding • Integration with LottoPGF: The platform is integrating LottoPGF
 that funds public goods via lottery. This feature will iterate from MVP to
 design and build the funding distribution mechanism which aims to rationalize
 resource distribution within the Zuzalu ecosystem, ensuring efficient and
 transparent allocation of funds.

 More Features are on the way!
