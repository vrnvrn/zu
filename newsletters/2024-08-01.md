 **﻿ZuForever**

 ZuForever V2 aims to address the recurring accommodation challenges faced by
 attendees and organizers of Zuzalu's pop-up cities. In the original model,
 ZuForever pooled funds to book townhouses for major web3 conferences, reducing
 costs through shared expenses and using NFTs to represent room bookings.
 However, this model's one-size-fits-all approach is ineffective for pop-up
 cities, where attendees have varied schedules and accommodation needs.

 ZuForever V2 proposes a more flexible, on-chain solution. It introduces wrapped
 NFTs representing available room days, connects staking pools with real-time
 data for accurate pricing and amenities, and automates KYC processes for
 confirmed attendees. This approach aims to reduce costs for attendees, ensure
 minimum attendance, and provide liquidity through a secondary market for NFTs.
 The project plans to finalize the V2 design, build an admin panel for NFT
 issuance, develop a bednight analytics interface, and form partnerships with
 pop-up cities, with key milestones set from September to Q1, 2025

 ⏩ ZuForever is a tool build for all Zuzalians, so please consider supporting us
