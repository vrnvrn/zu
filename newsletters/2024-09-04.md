# Zuzalu Community Newsletter | September 2024 Edition

 Happy September, everyone! I'm sorry about the delay with this month's
 newsletter – going directly from ZuVillage Georgia to the Zuitzerland off-site
 set me back a bit with tasks (but extending the Zu-immersion has been worth
 it)!

 Welcome to the **September 2024 Edition of the Zuzalu
 newsletter**, a short and sweet edition with Zuzalu.city tech updates,
 Agora City (formerly Zuzagora) trending topics, community accomplishments and
 reflections, and upcoming events.

 I'd also like to bring attention to the **Q2 anonymous feedback
 thread** so we can all assess the round and seek
 improvements for the future. Please take some time to share your insights!

## ## I. Zuzalu.City Software Updates

 🎉🎉 **Zuitzerland Kicks Off and Supports Alpha Testing on
 Zuzalu.city!Zuizterland** is now fully supporting the alpha testing of
 **Zuzalu.city**! They’re using the platform to ensure everything
 runs smoothly, helping us refine the user experience and making sure we meet
 the needs of our diverse communities. 🚀

 🚀 **Milestone Reached: ZuVillage Wraps Up, ZuCity Alpha Test
 Continues!** 🎉

 **@zuvillage**
 has successfully completed their part in the ZuCity Alpha test, marking a major
 milestone for us! Thanks to the valuable feedback from the ZuVillage community,
 we’ve made great progress. But the journey doesn’t stop here—the ZuCity alpha
 test continues as ZuCIty keeps iterating. 🙌

 **Zucity and Scroll made a huge progress on Scrollpass@Scroll_ZKP**
 and ZuCity have made great progress with ScrollPass! Event organizers can now
 easily create whitelist addresses, allowing qualified users to mint their SBT
 pass seamlessly, with ticket funds automatically transferred to the event’s
 multi-sig wallet.

 **ZuCity UI/UX improvement**

 Plus, ZuCity has rolled out some UI/UX improvements to make the experience even
 smoother. Community members can edit sessions after creation and export all
 sessions with just a few clicks.
