## I. Zuzalu.City Software Updates

 We’ve been hard at work enhancing Zuzalu.city, and we're excited to share some
 key updates with you.

 1. **Zuzalu.city Beta is on the horizone!** We're in our final
 sprints to **launch our open Beta** for all to test and use. Our
 brand-new ticketing flow covers everything from applications to seamless ticket
 issuing and sales. Join our **Element** to
 join the conversation! 

 2. **New Event registration and Ticketing Flow**: We're levelling
 up our event system to give every community event total flexibility in managing
 different types of events, featuring:

 1. Creation of customizable ZK credentials and ticket sales with smart
 contracts,

 2. Automatic check-in access management with self-managed ZK-NFTs and legacy
 ZuPass integration,

 3. a simple event application form, with a web3 form on the way. 

 2. **Upgraded Ceramic Node**: We’ve just upgraded our Ceramic Node
 to a more stable version! 🚀 Data migration is currently in progress, and we’re
 inviting projects to take this opportunity to test Ceramic once the Beta is
 live. 

 3. **New Resources**: **ZuBuilder
 blog** and **GitHub
 link** have been added to ZuCity making it easier to access
 updates and contributions. We are also building out our **documentation**! 

 4. **Front-End Kit Discussion**: We're building out a front-end
 kit to streamline the integration process and make development smoother for
 applications joining Zuzalu.city. 

 5. **Main Site Version Launch**: The alpha build has been switched
 to our **main
 domain** for more robust testing and deployment.

 **Join our Element**
 and stay tuned for our upcoming Beta launch!
