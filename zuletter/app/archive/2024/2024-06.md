## II. Zuzalu.City

We are thrilled to share some of the latest developments and initiatives at Zuzalu City.

🌟 Introducing ZuChat! We are excited to introduce ZuChat, now available on YouTube, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify. Hosted by Zuzalu.City, ZuChat brings together the builders, dreamers, and doers of ZuVillages for insightful discussions on topics ranging from technology to community governance.

So far, three episodes are live:

1. [Co-Building Zuzalu.City with Lemonade, with KC from lemonade.](https://www.guilded.gg/Zuzalu/blog/Community-Blog/Co-Building-ZuzaluCity-with-Lemonade)

2. [Enhancing Sustainability Through Rational Resources Allocation, with Daniela from Blockful and Nico from LottoPGF.](https://www.guilded.gg/Zuzalu/blog/Community-Blog/Enhancing-Sustainability-Through-Rational-Resources-Allocation)

3. [The Development Process of Zuzalu.City and Carbonvote, with Reno from ECF.](https://www.guilded.gg/Zuzalu/blog/Community-Blog/The-Development-Process-of-ZuzaluCity-and-Carbonvote)

As a community-driven initiative, we want your input. What topics are you passionate about? What issues deserve more attention? Join the conversation and help us shape a decentralized, community-driven podcast. Join ZuChat on on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwkedy0adBUNcL4qMSR34Ksa52ZRg-AKH&feature=shared), [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/zuchat/id1746537943), and [Spotify](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/zuzalucity).

🚀 ZuCity MVP Development. The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of Zuzalu.City is under development and will be live soon. Stay tuned for more updates!

🎤 ZuCity at ETHNA. ZuCity recently gave a speech at ETHNA on "How Zuzalu.City Can Empower Communities in Italy." This talk shared potential of ZuCity to enhance network states and empower local communities in Italy. Stay tuned for the video link when it becomes available!

💚 Giveth QF for ZuCity. ZuCity recently participated in the Giveth QF round, which has just concluded. A huge shoutout to everyone who supported us during this round and to LottoPGF for nominating ZuCity. Your contributions are greatly appreciated!

💗 Building Reputation and Trust with Blockful. Blockful and Zuzalu.city are discussing creating attestations and localized reputations, allowing users to issue and verify score badges to present their reputation. More collabs are on the way!

Together, we are building a vibrant, innovative, and collaborative community.
