## I. Zuzalu.City Software Updates

🎉🎉 **Zuitzerland Kicks Off and Supports Alpha Testing on Zuzalu.city!**
**Zuizterland** is now fully supporting the alpha testing of **Zuzalu.city**! They're using the platform to ensure everything runs smoothly, helping us refine the user experience and making sure we meet the needs of our diverse communities. 🚀
🚀 **Milestone Reached: ZuVillage Wraps Up, ZuCity Alpha Test Continues!** 🎉
[[\@zuvillage]](https://x.com/zuvillage) has successfully completed their part in the ZuCity Alpha test, marking a major milestone for us! Thanks to the valuable feedback from the ZuVillage community, we've made great progress. But the journey doesn't stop here---the ZuCity alpha test continues as ZuCIty keeps iterating. 🙌
**Zucity and Scroll made a huge progress on Scrollpass**
[[\@Scroll_ZKP]](https://x.com/Scroll_ZKP) and ZuCity have made great progress with ScrollPass! Event organizers can now easily create whitelist addresses, allowing qualified users to mint their SBT pass seamlessly, with ticket funds automatically transferred to the event's multi-sig wallet.

**ZuCity UI/UX improvement**
Plus, ZuCity has rolled out some UI/UX improvements to make the experience even smoother. Community members can edit sessions after creation and export all sessions with just a few clicks.
