## I. Zuzalu.City Software Updates

We've been hard at work enhancing Zuzalu.city, and we're excited to share some key updates with you.
1\. **Zuzalu.city Beta is on the horizone!** We're in our final sprints to **launch our open Beta** for all to test and use. Our brand-new ticketing flow covers everything from applications to seamless ticket issuing and sales. Join our [[Element]](https://matrix.to/#/#zuzalusoftware:matrix.org) to join the conversation!
2\. **New Event registration and Ticketing Flow**: We're levelling up our event system to give every community event total flexibility in managing different types of events, featuring:
1\. Creation of customizable ZK credentials and ticket sales with smart contracts,
2\. Automatic check-in access management with self-managed ZK-NFTs and legacy ZuPass integration,
3\. a simple event application form, with a web3 form on the way.

2\. **Upgraded Ceramic Node**: We've just upgraded our Ceramic Node to a more stable version! 🚀

Data migration is currently in progress, and we're inviting projects to take this opportunity to test Ceramic once the Beta is live.
3\. **New Resources**: [[ZuBuilder blog]](https://zuzalu.craft.me/ZuBuilderBlog) and [[GitHub link]](https://github.com/EthereumCommunityFund/Zuzalu-City)have been added to ZuCity making it easier to access updates and contributions. We are also building out our [documentation](https://zuzalu.gitbook.io/zuzalucitybeta)!
4\. **Front-End Kit Discussion**: We're building out a front-end kit to streamline the integration process and make development smoother for applications joining Zuzalu.city.
5\. **Main Site Version Launch**: The alpha build has been switched to our [[main domain]](https://zuzalu.city/) for more robust testing and deployment.
